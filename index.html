Crucial Alps Travel Notes & Party Logistics

🚘 Car Rental for 4 Adults

For 4 adults + luggage traveling through the Dolomites, request a full-size SUV (Audi Q7/BMW X5) or passenger minivan (Mercedes V-Class), or split into 2 compact wagons for easier mountain hairpin driving.

🚠 Gondola Closing Strictness

Dolomites and Swiss cable cars adhere to strict timetables. Never cut it close to final descent (Piz La Ila last descent 5:00 PM, Boé 5:30 PM). Mountain descents on foot take 3+ hours!

💶 Currency & Official Bank ATM Strategy

EU uses Euro (€); Switzerland uses Swiss Franc (CHF). Contactless card/Apple Pay is accepted almost everywhere. For cash (bakeries, lockers, remote huts):
• Euros: Deutsche Bank Filiale (Schwanthalerstraße 32) is 2 doors down from Hotel Germania in Munich (1-min walk).
• Swiss Francs: UBS Switzerland (Plazza da Scoula 10, St. Moritz or Bahnhofstrasse 37, Zermatt).
• USAA Debit Tip: Official bank branches charge $0.00 local fee; USAA charges a 1% foreign fee (~$1.65 on €150). ALWAYS choose "Without Conversion / Settle in EUR or CHF" to decline dynamic conversion markup! Avoid standalone Euronet kiosks.

🌤 September Mountain Weather

Early September brings crisp, clear days, but high summits (2,500m - 3,000m) can drop near freezing with sudden wind shifts. Always layer with lightweight fleece and a wind/rain shell in your daypack.

Foreign Currency & Banking Strategy USAA & Travel Debit Guide
Official Bank Branches & Recommended ATMs (Euros & Swiss Francs)

Stick to official commercial bank branches (24/7 self-service indoor lobbies). Foreign bank owner fee is $0.00, and USAA only assesses a 1% foreign transaction fee (~$1.65 per €150). Decline Dynamic Currency Conversion (DCC): always choose "Without Conversion / Charge in EUR or CHF"!

🇩🇪 Euros in Munich: Deutsche Bank Filiale 1-Min Walk

Location: Schwanthalerstraße 32, 80336 München (just 2 doors down / 40m right of Boutique Hotel Germania front entrance).

Why here: 24/7 self-service indoor ATM lobby. Major German retail bank. Safe, well-lit, $0 local ATM surcharge. €100–€200 cash is ideal for bakeries, coin lockers, and mountain rifugi.
